# Northstar Maps Launches CoolRoute for Hot-Weather Navigation

Barcelona, July 24, 2026 — Northstar Maps today launched CoolRoute, a navigation feature designed to help pedestrians find more comfortable routes during periods of extreme heat.

CoolRoute considers tree cover, public drinking fountains, shaded streets, indoor passageways, and designated cooling centers when suggesting a walking route. A recommended route may take several minutes longer than the fastest option but can reduce the amount of time spent in direct sunlight.

The feature also adds a temporary map category that highlights drinking-water points, cooling centers, public parks, and air-conditioned public buildings. Availability varies by city and depends on information supplied by municipal partners and local organizations.

Northstar Maps is initially testing CoolRoute in Barcelona, Madrid, Valencia, Seville, and Lisbon. The company plans to expand the feature based on user feedback and the availability of local data.

“Navigation should respond not only to where people are going, but also to the conditions in which they are travelling,” said Elena Martín, Head of Urban Mobility at Northstar Maps.

CoolRoute is available from today in the latest version of the Northstar Maps mobile application.
